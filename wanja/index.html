About

Elegance, fashion and real brand experiences

Wanja Mwangi is a visual storyteller and creative muse based in Nairobi, Kenya. She models for fashion and commercial shoots, creates content for brands, and appears at events and activations.

In the studio she balances softness and strength. On location she makes cafés, rooftops and venues feel aspirational yet approachable. For brands, she lets the product lead while her presence adds personality.
