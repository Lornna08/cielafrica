About

Wanja Mwangi

Visual storyteller and creative muse, based in Nairobi, Kenya.

01 / In brief

Blending elegance, fashion and authentic brand experiences.

Wanja Mwangi works in front of the camera and behind the content: modelling for fashion and commercial shoots, creating and marketing content for brands, reviewing products and services, and appearing at events and brand activations.

Her work moves between concept-driven studio editorials and real places, from rooftop cafés to city streets. On location she makes a venue feel aspirational yet approachable. In the studio she balances softness and strength. For brands such as Tribe Nomad, she lets the product lead while her presence adds personality and authenticity.
