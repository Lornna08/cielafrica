Wanja Mwangi

  • Visual storyteller
  • Model
  • Creative muse

Wanja Mwangi is a visual storyteller and creative muse based in Nairobi, Kenya, blending elegance, fashion and authentic brand experiences.

She works in front of the camera and behind the content: modelling for fashion and commercial shoots, creating and marketing content for brands, reviewing products and services, and appearing at events and brand activations.

In the studio she balances softness and strength, creating refined images that feel both luxurious and authentic. On location she brings effortless confidence to cafés, rooftops, city streets and events, making a venue feel aspirational yet approachable. For brands, she lets the product lead while her presence adds personality.
