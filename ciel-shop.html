Questions.

Something else on your mind? Ask us

Is the template the real software?

Yes. It’s the actual Ciel Shop interface with every module working — orders reserve stock, shipping consumes batches, receiving purchase orders adds stock. It runs on a sample dataset stored only in your browser, so you can’t break anything, and “Reset template” restores it.

Does it work with M-Pesa?

Ciel Shop records payments by method, including M-Pesa. Live M-Pesa (Daraja) collection and reconciliation is connected during deployment for your own paybill or till — it isn’t active in the public template.

Can I bring my existing products and stock?

Yes. During setup we import your catalogue, opening stock per location, suppliers and customers from spreadsheets or your current system, and check the numbers with you before go-live.

How does expiry tracking work?

Turn it on per product. Each delivery is recorded as a batch with an expiry date, and orders are picked first-expiry-first-out. Batches inside your warning window are flagged, and expired stock can be written off in one step with a ledger entry.

Can several people use it, and can I see who did what?

Yes. Every stock movement, order change and refund is recorded with the person responsible. Production deployments include sign-in and role-based permissions.

Who owns the data?

You do. Whether we host it or it runs on your own server, your data is yours and can be exported at any time — every report already exports to CSV.

How long does it take to go live?

It depends on how much data and how many integrations you need. Tell us about your business through the quote form and we’ll come back with a timeline alongside the price.

Ready to run your shop from one place?

Tell us about your products, locations and channels. We’ll come back with a setup plan and a quote.
