Refund and Returns Policy

When you can cancel an order, get a refund or a reprint, and how to ask.

Last updated 8 November 2026

In short

  • Printed photo books are made to order. You can cancel for a full refund before printing starts. After that, we reprint or refund only if the book arrives damaged or faulty.
  • Digital products and print-ready PDFs can’t be returned once delivered, unless the file is faulty and we can’t fix it.
  • Domain names are refunded if they can’t be registered. Once a domain is registered in your name, it can’t be refunded.
  • Projects follow the payment and cancellation terms in your proposal.

Printed photo books

Cancelling. You can cancel your order for a full refund at any time before we start printing. Once printing has started, the book is personal to you, so it can’t be cancelled or refunded if you change your mind.

Damaged, faulty or not as approved. If your book arrives damaged, has a printing or binding fault, or doesn’t match the design you approved, tell us within 7 days of receiving it. Send your order reference and photos of the problem. We’ll reprint the book at no cost, or refund you in full if you prefer. You don’t need to send the book back unless we ask.

What isn’t a fault:

  • anything that was in the design you approved, such as spelling, crops, empty pages or low-resolution photos the editor warned you about;
  • small differences in colour between your screen and print.

Lost in delivery. If your book doesn’t arrive and the courier confirms it is lost, we’ll reprint it or refund you in full.

Digital products and print-ready PDFs

Digital products and print-ready PDFs are delivered by email straight after payment, so they can’t be returned. If a file is faulty, or isn’t what was described, tell us within 7 days. We’ll fix it or send a working file, and if we can’t, we’ll refund you.

Domain names

If a domain can’t be registered for any reason, you get a full refund of what you paid for it, or another domain at the same price if you prefer. Once a domain is registered in your name, the registration can’t be cancelled or refunded. Our Domain terms have the details.

Projects and services

Deposits, payment stages and cancellation are set out in your proposal. If a project is cancelled, you pay for the work done up to that point. If you’ve paid for work we haven’t done, we refund that part.

Paid twice, or paid the wrong amount

If you were charged twice for the same order, or paid more than the order total, we refund the extra in full once we’ve matched the payment.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go to the M-Pesa number you paid from, unless we agree another way with you. We aim to send a refund within 7 working days of approving it. How quickly it reaches you then depends on the payment provider. We’ll tell you when it has been sent.

How to ask

Email hello@cielafrica.com or WhatsApp +254 117 959 105. Please include your order reference.

Your rights

This policy doesn’t limit any rights you have under consumer protection law.
