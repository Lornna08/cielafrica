Product

Real accounting underneath. A calm workspace on top.

C-Ledger is built around a genuine double-entry ledger. Invoices, bills, expenses and bank activity all post balanced journal entries — so every report is derived from the same source of truth.

Double-entry at the core

Every transaction posts equal debits and credits. Unbalanced entries cannot be posted, and account balances are computed from posted lines only.

Every number traces back

Click any balance on a report to see the ledger lines behind it, then the journal, then the invoice, bill or bank line that created it.

Controls built for teams

Role-based permissions, approval steps, closed periods and an audit trail of who did what — without slowing down day-to-day work.

One overview of the business

Cash across every account, income against expenses, receivables and payables, and the items that need attention today — calculated live from the ledger for the period you choose.

  • Cash position, net profit and month-so-far at a glance
  • Cash flow, expense breakdown and revenue trend charts
  • Overdue invoices, bills due and unreconciled lines flagged
General ledger

Posted means permanent. Corrections leave a trail.

C-Ledger treats the general ledger the way an auditor expects: drafts can change, posted entries cannot. Mistakes are corrected with a dated reversal, never by quietly editing history.

  • Draft, then post

    Save entries as drafts, attach supporting documents, and post only when debits equal credits. The post button stays disabled until they do.

  • Reverse, don’t edit

    A posted entry is corrected with a linked reversing entry on a date you choose — with the reason recorded and both entries visible.

  • Close the books

    Close a month once it’s reviewed. Nothing can post into a closed period until someone with permission reopens it — and that’s logged too.

  • A chart of accounts that fits

    Assets, liabilities, equity, income and expenses with sub-accounts and control accounts. Accounts with history can be archived, never deleted.

Everyday workflows

Invoices, bills and expenses that post themselves.

Your team works with familiar documents. C-Ledger writes the journal entries in the background — correctly, every time.

Invoicing and receivables

Create invoices with line discounts and VAT, issue them to post to receivables, and record full or partial payments against them. Overpayments are held as customer credit, not lost.

  • Draft, sent, partially paid, paid, overdue and void statuses

Bills with an approval step

Supplier bills move from draft to approved to posted. Pay them in full or in part from any bank or mobile money account.

Expense claims, reviewed

Staff submit expenses with receipts. Approvers approve or reject with a reason, and approved claims post with VAT backed out.

Banking across every account

Operating, reserve, petty cash and mobile money in one place, with running statements and transfers that post to both sides automatically.

FAQ

Questions finance teams ask us.

Is the template the real software?

Yes. It’s the same application and accounting engine, loaded with twelve months of sample books for a fictional design studio. Everything you post is stored only in your browser, and “Reset template” restores the original books.

Is it real double-entry accounting?

Yes. Every invoice, bill, expense, payment and transfer posts a balanced journal entry to the general ledger. Reports are computed from posted ledger lines, and built-in checks confirm the trial balance agrees and the balance sheet balances.

Can we edit a transaction after it’s posted?

Not directly — that’s deliberate. Posted entries are corrected with a linked reversal (or by voiding the source invoice or bill), so your history stays complete and auditable. Drafts can be edited freely.

Does it handle VAT?

Tax rates are configurable, and VAT on sales and purchases posts to a tax account automatically. The template is set up with Kenyan VAT at 16%. C-Ledger does not file returns for you — confirm your tax treatment with your accountant.

Can it connect to our bank or M-Pesa?

Bank statements can be imported as CSV today. Direct bank or M-Pesa feeds are available as a quoted integration during setup. The public template never connects to a bank or moves money.

Who owns our data?

You do. Whether we host it or you do, your books stay in your organisation’s database and can be exported at any time.
