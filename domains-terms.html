Domain terms

Version 2026-10-24. These terms apply to domain names ordered on cielafrica.com/domains from Ciel Africa (“we”, “us”). They sit alongside our general terms and privacy policy.

1. Ordering and payment

  • You pay for your domain when you order, by M-Pesa.
  • Once your payment is received, your domain is registered in the registrant details you give at checkout, and we email you the details.
  • A search result doesn’t reserve a domain. A domain can be registered by someone else until your payment is complete.
  • Registration is subject to each extension’s rules and eligibility requirements (for example, registrant details for .ke domains).

2. Prices, taxes and renewals

  • Prices are in Kenyan shillings (KES). The total shown at checkout is what you pay for the term you choose: the first-year price plus the renewal price for each additional year.
  • Any tax that applies is shown at checkout. There are no other fees.
  • Renewal is separate from the first registration and is charged at the renewal price in force at the time. Renewal prices are shown next to first-year prices. Nothing is renewed or charged automatically; we’ll contact you before expiry.
  • Any change to your domain or its price requires your agreement.

3. Refunds

  • If a domain can’t be registered for any reason, you get a full refund of the amount you paid for it to the M-Pesa number you paid from. If you prefer, you can choose another domain at the same price instead.
  • Refund timing depends on the payment provider. We’ll tell you when your refund is sent.
  • Once a domain is registered in your name, the registration can’t be cancelled or refunded.

4. Your details and ownership

  • The domain is registered using the registrant details you provide. Make sure they are accurate and belong to the person or organisation that should own the domain.
  • You must not order a domain that infringes someone else’s trademark or is intended to impersonate another person or organisation.

5. Data we keep

  • We collect only what’s needed to register the domain and support you: name, email, phone, address, town, country and, optionally, your organisation.
  • We never see or store your M-Pesa PIN or card details.
  • Unpaid orders are deleted after 30 days. Paid orders are kept for 5 years for accounting and tax records, then deleted.
  • To ask for a copy or correction of your data, email hello@cielafrica.com with your order reference.

6. Contact

Email hello@cielafrica.com or WhatsApp +254 706 549 703, quoting your order reference (it starts with “CD-”).
