Our kitchen

Everything starts with the fire.

We cook almost everything over wood and charcoal: whole fish from the coast, goat from the Rift Valley, greens from a farm in Limuru that we visit every Monday.

The menu is short and it changes with what's good this week. Ask your server what came in this morning.
