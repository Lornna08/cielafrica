Questions, answered.

How do I pay?

With M-Pesa. Enter your Safaricom number at checkout and you’ll get a prompt on your phone — enter your PIN and you’re done. We never see your PIN.

What happens after I pay?

Your domain is registered in your name and we email you the details, together with your order reference.

How do renewals work?

Your payment covers the term you choose. Renewal is separate and is charged at the renewal price shown on this page — often different from the first year. We’ll contact you before your domain expires; nothing is charged automatically.

Who owns the domain?

You do. It’s registered with the details you give at checkout, so use the person or organisation that should own it.

Can I connect it to my website or email?

Yes. Send us your order reference and we’ll point it to your website, set up business email, or build a website on it for you.

What if something goes wrong?

If a domain can’t be registered for any reason, you get a full refund of what you paid for it.

The short version of our terms.

Plain language, before you pay. The full domain terms are one click away.

  • You pay when you order, by M-Pesa.
  • A search doesn’t reserve a domain — paying for it does.
  • The domain is registered in the details you give at checkout.
  • If a domain can’t be registered, you get a full refund of what you paid for it.
  • Renewals are separate from the first registration, and their prices are shown upfront.
  • Any change to your domain or its price needs your agreement.
