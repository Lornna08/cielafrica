How registration
actually works.

No robots pretending. A real person registers every domain, and you’re told the truth at each step.

  1. 01 · Search

    Check the registry

    We ask the official registry for each extension where it publishes one. It’s a strong signal, not a reservation — someone else could still register the name first.

  2. 02 · Order

    Pay, or confirm first

    Most domains you can pay for straight away with M-Pesa. For a few extensions, we confirm the name and price with the registry before charging you anything.

  3. 03 · Register

    We register it by hand

    After payment is confirmed, we check again and register the domain in your name through an accredited registrar — usually within one working day.

  4. 04 · Done

    Yours, or your money back

    You get an email when the registry confirms it. If it can’t be registered, you get a full refund — or an alternative at the same price if you choose.

Questions, answered.

Does searching reserve a domain for me?

No. Search results are preliminary. A domain is only reserved once it’s registered — until then, anyone in the world can register it. That’s why we register as soon as your payment is confirmed.

How long does registration take?

We register by hand, usually within one working day of your payment being confirmed. Some registries, including .ke, can take a little longer to confirm. We email you at every step.

What if the domain can’t be registered?

If a domain turns out to be taken, reserved or rejected by the registry, you’re eligible for a full refund of what you paid for it. If you’d rather have a similar name, we can register an alternative at the same agreed price — but only with your agreement.

Why do some extensions say “we confirm before you pay”?

Some extensions don’t publish a public registry lookup, some names are sold at premium prices the registry doesn’t show in a search, and some prices need checking with the registry. For those, you send a request first, we confirm the name and price, and only then send you a payment link.

How do renewals work?

Your first payment covers the term you choose. Renewal is separate and is charged at the renewal price shown on this page at the time — often higher than the first year. We’ll contact you before the domain expires; nothing is charged automatically.

Who owns the domain?

The domain is registered with the registrant details you give at checkout, so use the person or organisation that should own it.

Can I transfer a domain or use my own nameservers?

Yes — contact us with your order reference and we’ll set your nameservers or guide you through a transfer. These are handled by our team rather than automatically.

How can I pay?

With M-Pesa through a prompt on your phone. Card payment appears at checkout when it’s available. We never see or store your card details or M-Pesa PIN.

Where does the availability information come from?

For most extensions we ask the official registry’s RDAP service (for example Verisign for .com and KeNIC for .co.ke). For .co, .io and .me there’s no public RDAP service, so we check public DNS — which can prove a name is taken, but not that it’s free — and confirm those by hand.
