Questions

Pricing & process.

How is pricing calculated?

Every project is different. Pricing depends on design, functionality, integrations, content, complexity and requirements. Figures are honest starting points, not fixed quotes — request a quote for your specific project.

What's included?

Discovery and strategy, UX/UI design, development, testing, deployment and a support window. Exact deliverables are agreed up front.

Do you work internationally?

Yes — with startups, SMEs, corporations and institutions locally and worldwide. Pricing is in USD.

Can you take over an existing project?

Often. We can audit an existing codebase or site, then improve, extend or rebuild depending on its condition and your goals.

What happens after launch?

Ongoing maintenance, security monitoring, bug fixing and improvements so your product keeps running well.
