Questions

Common questions about services & pricing.

How is pricing calculated?

Every project is different. Pricing depends on scope, functionality, complexity, integrations, timeline and requirements. The figures shown are honest starting points, not fixed quotes — request a quote and we'll scope your specific project.

What's included in a typical engagement?

Discovery and strategy, UX/UI design, development, testing, deployment and a support window. Exact deliverables are agreed up front so there are no surprises.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. We work with startups, SMEs, corporations and institutions locally and internationally. Pricing is in USD.

Can you take over an existing project?

Often, yes. We can audit an existing codebase or site, then improve, extend or rebuild it depending on its condition and your goals.

What happens after launch?

We offer ongoing maintenance, security monitoring, bug fixing and improvements so your product keeps running well and evolving.
