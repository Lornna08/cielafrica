Software, considered.

Ciel Africa is a software engineering and digital product studio founded and led by Lorna Matu. We help businesses and institutions turn ideas into software that's genuinely useful, carefully designed and built to scale.

The studio

Ciel — French for "sky" — is about altitude and clear air: rising above the noise to build software that feels calm, considered and dependable. We work with startups, SMEs, corporations and institutions, locally in Kenya and internationally.

Whether it's a website, a mobile app, a full ERP, an AI automation or a business system, the same principle holds: understand the problem deeply, then engineer something that solves it well and lasts.

Custom, not templated

We build software shaped around how your business actually works — not forced into a generic mold.

Design and engineering as one craft

We don't treat design as decoration applied after the code. They're the same act of making something well.

Built to scale, made to last

Clean architecture and maintainable code, so your product keeps working as you grow.

Clear, honest communication

Plain language, steady updates, and realistic timelines from first call to launch and beyond.
