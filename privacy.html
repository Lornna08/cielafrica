Privacy Policy

How Ciel Africa handles the information you share with us. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

When you contact us or request a quote, we collect the details you provide — your name, email, company and project information — so we can respond and work with you.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to you, prepare proposals, and deliver work you've asked for. We don't sell your data.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers needed to run our business (for example, email and hosting), and only as far as necessary.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time by emailing info@cielafrica.com.
