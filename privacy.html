Who we are

Ciel Africa (“we”, “us”) runs cielafrica.com. We are responsible for the personal information described in this policy. We handle it in line with the Data Protection Act, 2019. Questions about your information go to hello@cielafrica.com.

What we collect

  • When you contact us or ask for a quote: your name, email address, phone number, company and the project details you share.
  • When you buy something: your name, email address and phone number, your delivery address for printed books, what you ordered and how much you paid, and the M-Pesa confirmation for your payment. We never see or store your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Domain names: the registrant details the domain needs (name, email, phone, address, town, country and, if you give it, your organisation).
  • Email updates: your email address, your first name if you give it, where you signed up and when you confirmed.
  • WhatsApp: the messages you send us there.
  • Visits to the website: anonymous statistics, such as which pages are visited and the type of device. See our Cookie Policy.

Ciel Photo Books

When you design a photo book, your photos and your book are kept in your own browser, on your device. So that ordering is quick, the finished pages of a book that has photos (not your photo library) are uploaded in the background to private storage while you design. Only Ciel Africa can open them. They are deleted when you delete the book, and pages of books that are never ordered are deleted after 30 days.

Title and style suggestions use only the text you type. If an AI provider is switched on, that text (never your photos) is sent to it to make suggestions.

For printed books, the print-ready files of the version you approved are sent to us after payment. They are used only to print your order, and deleted once the order is complete. We never use your photos in galleries, marketing or AI training.

How we use your information

  • To reply to you, prepare proposals and deliver the work you ask for.
  • To take payment, fulfil and deliver your order, and send you receipts and order updates.
  • To register and manage domain names you order.
  • To send our email updates, if you have subscribed.
  • To keep the website secure and to understand, in aggregate, how it is used so we can improve it.
  • To keep the records the law requires, for example for tax and accounting.

We use your information because we need it to provide what you asked for, because you agreed (for example, to email updates), because the law requires it, or for our legitimate interest in running a safe, working business. We don’t sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the service providers we need to run our business, and only as much as they need:

  • hosting and storage for the website and orders;
  • our email provider, for receipts, order updates and email updates;
  • Safaricom, to process M-Pesa payments;
  • couriers and riders, to deliver printed books;
  • domain registries and registrars, to register domains;
  • an AI provider, for title suggestions, when it is switched on (your text only).

We may also share information when the law requires it. Some providers store information outside Kenya. When they do, we use providers that protect it to the standard the law requires.

How long we keep it

  • Messages and quote requests: as long as we need them to reply and to work with you.
  • Orders and payment records: up to 5 years, for accounting and tax.
  • Unpaid domain orders: deleted after 30 days.
  • Photo book pages: as described above.
  • Email subscribers: until you unsubscribe.

Keeping it safe

We protect your information with secure connections (HTTPS), private storage and access limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct anything that is wrong;
  • delete your information, where we don’t have to keep it by law;
  • stop using it for a purpose, or stop sending you email updates;
  • move it to another provider, where that applies.

Email hello@cielafrica.com and we’ll respond within the time the law sets. You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

Email updates

We send a confirmation email first and add you only once you tap its link. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works in one click. You can also reply “unsubscribe” or email hello@cielafrica.com.

Children

Our website and services are meant for adults. If you put photos of children in a photo book, you confirm that you have the right to do so, for example as their parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change affects you significantly, we’ll tell you.
