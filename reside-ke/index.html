How it works

The way agencies already work — minus the chasing.

Capture the lead

Website, portal, WhatsApp or walk-in — every enquiry lands with an owner and a follow-up date.

Book the viewing

Schedule against the property and contact. The calendar keeps agents and owners in step.

Negotiate the offer

Log offers and counters on the deal. The pricing tool shows where the number sits against the market.

Sign the contract

Move the deal to Contract, attach the documents and track completion dates.

Record the commission

Closing a deal records commission as earned — then approved and paid, per agent.

FAQ

Questions agencies ask us.

Is the template the real software?

Yes. It’s the same application, loaded with sample data for a fictional agency. Anything you change is stored only in your browser, and “Reset template” restores the original data.

Are the properties, people and prices real?

No. Listings, contacts, valuations and transactions in the template are synthetic. Property photography is from Unsplash. The market estimate in the pricing tool is sample data, not an independent valuation.

Does it handle both sales and rentals?

Yes — sales deals with offers and commission, and rentals with leases, rent records, applications and maintenance requests.

Can it connect to M-Pesa, property portals or our email?

These integrations are available as add-ons and are configured during setup. They are not connected in the public template, which never moves money or sends messages.

How long does setup take?

A standard branded deployment is typically ready within days. Data migration and integrations depend on your existing systems — we’ll give you a timeline with the quote.

Who owns our data?

You do. On a managed or self-hosted deployment, your data stays in your agency’s database and can be exported at any time.
