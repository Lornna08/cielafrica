Save & publish

Publishing: once the one-click setup is done (GitHub token + password added in Vercel — see the README), clicking Save commits straight to your repo and the change goes live in ~30 seconds. No downloads.

If the setup isn't in place yet, Save still works — it just downloads the file instead, and you upload it to your repo manually. Either way your edits are kept in this browser until you publish.
